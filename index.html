What Can You Report?

Help us identify the most pressing issues in your neighborhood.

Garbage and Waste

Report overflowing bins and uncollected waste in public areas.

Road Damage

Report potholes, broken roads, and unsafe streets causing commuter issues.

Broken Streetlights

Report streetlights that need repair to ensure neighborhood safety.

Water Leakage

Report leaking public pipes, broken mains, and water wastage.

Drainage Problems

Report blocked drains, overflowing sewers, and flooding issues.

Public Space Issues

Report problems in parks, damaged sidewalks, and other public areas.
