ABOUT FIXMYAREA

About FixMyArea

A simple platform for reporting and tracking local community problems.

The Problem

Local problems such as potholes, garbage, broken streetlights, drainage problems and water leakage can affect everyday life. FixMyArea provides a simple way to organize these reports.

Our Solution

Citizens can report local problems and browse issues reported by the community. Each issue contains information about its location, category and current status.
